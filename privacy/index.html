Privacy Policy .

alvvo — an AI automation agency operated by Almoayad Alriyami, Barka, Sultanate of Oman.
Last updated: 5 July 2026.

alvvo builds and operates AI-powered software for businesses — including automation, dashboards, websites, apps, and messaging assistants such as WhatsApp booking and customer-service bots. This policy explains what information we process, why, how we protect it, and how you can have it deleted. It applies to our software and to our integrations with the WhatsApp Business Platform.

Information we process

When you message a business whose assistant we operate on the WhatsApp Business Platform, we process:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile display name, as provided to us by the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • The content of the messages you send and receive in that conversation — text and any media you attach (for example, a payment or booking screenshot).
  • Business profile and conversation details — such as the dates, party size, service, or enquiry you describe.
  • Operational metadata — message timestamps and delivery/read status — used to run the service reliably.

We do not collect your WhatsApp contacts, your location, your browsing history, or any data beyond the conversation you chose to have with a business.

How and why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry, check availability, quote prices, and help complete a booking or request on behalf of the business you contacted.
  • To pass your request and its details to that business so it can fulfil it.
  • To maintain conversation context so you don't repeat yourself, and to hand the chat to a human when needed.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Our role

For each business we serve, that business is the controller of its own customer conversations and instructs us how to handle them; alvvo acts as its service provider. WhatsApp/Meta act as a processor, transmitting messages on behalf of and at the instruction of the business, under the WhatsApp Business Data Processing Terms.

Who we share it with

  • The business you messaged — the venue or client whose WhatsApp line you contacted.
  • Meta Platforms — message delivery runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API).
  • Infrastructure and AI providers we contract to operate the assistant (secure hosting and language-model processing), only to provide the service.

We do not sell, rent, or license your personal information, we do not use your messages to serve advertising, and we do not use your data for eligibility or profiling decisions.

Retention

We keep conversation records only as long as needed to provide the service and for the business's reasonable record-keeping, then delete or anonymise them. Note that the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) itself retains message data for a maximum of 30 days for transmission purposes. You can request deletion of your data at any time — see Data deletion.

Security

WhatsApp messages travel end-to-end encrypted over the official Meta Cloud API. Access tokens and credentials are stored securely and restricted to the systems that need them. We limit access to conversation data to what is necessary to operate the service.

Your rights and choices

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your conversation data at any time.
  • You can stop messaging the business's WhatsApp line, and block the number in WhatsApp, whenever you wish.
  • To exercise any right, see Data deletion or contact us below; we respond promptly.

Children

Our services are for businesses and their adult customers and are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Changes

We may update this policy as our services evolve; material changes will be reflected here with a revised date.
