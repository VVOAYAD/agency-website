Data Deletion .

How to have your data deleted from alvvo. Last updated: 5 July 2026.

You can ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you — your WhatsApp phone number, your display name, and the content of your conversation with a business whose assistant we operate — at any time, free of charge.

How to request deletion

  1. Email alvvoayad@gmail.com with the subject line "Data deletion request".
  2. Include the WhatsApp phone number you used to message the business, so we can locate your data.
  3. Tell us the business you contacted, if you know it (for example, the venue or chalet).
We verify the request comes from you (the owner of that phone number), delete your conversation data from our systems, and confirm by email — normally within 30 days. If we cannot delete certain data for a legitimate legal reason, we will explain why.

Other ways

  • You can also ask the business you messaged directly to delete your conversation; as the controller of its customer data, it can instruct us to remove it.
  • You can stop messaging the business and block its number in WhatsApp at any time.
